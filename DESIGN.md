## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an editorial, structured, and distinguished ethos designed for ambitious graduates and early-career seekers navigating career inflection points. It combines the rigorous prestige of collegiate academia with modern, outcome-driven tech mentorship.

### Personality & Tone
- **Authoritative yet Nurturing:** Communicates deep institutional credibility, clarity, and genuine investment in the mentee's trajectory.
- **Calm, High-Signal Clarity:** Removes career-search anxiety through deliberate whitespace, structured roadmaps, and uncompromised hierarchy.
- **Modern Editorial Distinction:** Leverages rich, ink-like forest tones paired with tactile warm paper backgrounds, steering clear of disposable boot-camp tropes.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Style Blend:** Modern Editorial Minimalism combined with structured architectural grid work.
- **Surfaces & Atmosphere:** Warm cream tactile surfaces replacing sterile cool whites, bounded by whisper-thin warm slate borders and grounded by deep pine structural anchors.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classic editorial institutions, tailored for focused digital readability.

### Key Roles
- **Primary (`#073F30` - Deep Forest Pine):** Used for primary conversion touchpoints, high-impact headlines, deep container headers, and institutional anchoring.
- **Secondary (`#0D5C46` - Mid Pine):** Active interaction states, secondary buttons, timeline progress nodes, and key narrative markers.
- **Tertiary (`#C98A3C` - Burnished Amber / Ochre):** Micro-highlights, selective urgency badges, cohort milestone flags, and pilot status indicators.
- **Neutral Dark (`#18221E` / `#2D3748`):** Deep charcoal ink tones for pristine typographic contrast without harsh absolute blacks.
- **Background Base (`#FBFAF7`):** Primary canvas tone providing an organic, warm ivory surface.
- **Surface Muted (`#F4F1EA`):** Tinted structural containers, roadmaps, comparison table headers, and alternating sections.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E5E0D5` / `#D4CEBF`):** Warm slate-parchment hairline rules that maintain rigorous structure without visual noise.

## Typography

The type scale balances the sharp architectural restraint of **DM Sans** for headlines and interactive elements with the humanized legibility of **Manrope** for long-form narrative body and tabular data.

### Hierarchy & Typesetting Rules
- **Display & Section Titles:** Set in DM Sans with negative tracking to emulate tight, premium editorial publication formatting.
- **Body & Explanatory Prose:** Set in Manrope at 15px/24px or 18px/28px to ensure effortless tracking during curriculum inspection.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or small title caps set with positive tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) in DM Sans for date ranges, cohort badges, and timeline indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid anchors the experience across desktop viewpoints, reflowing to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (min-width: 1024px):** Max container width of `1200px` centered on the viewport. Sections maintain generous vertical breathing room (`space-2xl`) to avoid sensory overload.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Compacted grid gutters (`1.5rem`), transitioning multi-column matrices (like curriculum modules) to 2-column stacked pairings.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with edge bleed support for key phase cards and horizontal scroll tabs for the 50-day schedule. Outer canvas padding is locked at `1.25rem`.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section blocks adhere strictly to multiples of 8px. Spacing between independent content modules defaults to `space-xl` (40px) or `space-2xl` (64px) to preserve editorial pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic floating elevations and loud drop shadows. Instead, structural hierarchy relies on low-contrast framing and tactile surface layering.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas sits at `#FBFAF7`. Secondary surfaces (cards, sidebars, roadmap paths) utilize `#F4F1EA` or crisp white `#FFFFFF` layered over the ivory canvas.
- **Tactile Ghost Borders:** Cards and containers use a crisp `1px solid #E5E0D5` stroke. 
- **Subtle Grounding Shadow:** On critical floating items (pricing pilot highlight or active popovers), elevation uses a diffused, warm pine-tinted ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(7, 63, 48, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(24, 34, 30, 0.03);`
- **Active / Hover Transitions:** Elements do not lift aggressively into z-space; they respond with border tone shifts to Mid Pine (`#0D5C46`) and subtle background warmths.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a deliberate "Soft" posture (Level 1), balancing contemporary interface softness with the crisp corners of classic editorial design.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Accordion Headers):** `0.25rem` (4px) to preserve crisp architectural precision.
- **Structural Cards & Modules (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px) for modular curriculum cards, timeline blocks, and comparison containers.
- **Hero Banners & Pricing Enclosures (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px) for major framing bounds.
- **Badges & Micro-tags:** Fully pill-rounded (`9999px`) to create an organic silhouette contrast against the structured square cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#073F30`, text `#FBFAF7`, border `1px solid transparent`, radius `4px`. Hover deepens tone to `#0D5C46`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Background `transparent`, text `#073F30`, border `1px solid #073F30`. Hover applies `#F4F1EA`.
- **Accent Action:** Background `#C98A3C`, text `#FFFFFF`, used strictly for limited-seat cohort pilot applications.

### Chips & Badges
- **Cohort Badge:** Pill-shaped, subtle cream background `#F4F1EA`, border `1px solid #E5E0D5`, text `#18221E` (DM Sans Label-SM, uppercase).
- **Status Indicator (e.g., "50-Day Sprint"):** Background `rgba(201, 138, 60, 0.12)`, text `#94611F`, border `1px solid rgba(201, 138, 60, 0.25)`.

### Cards
- **Standard Card:** Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F4F1EA`, radius `8px`, border `1px solid #E5E0D5`, internal padding `space-lg`.
- **Selected / Featured Card (Transparent Pricing):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `2px solid #073F30`, subtle pine ambient shadow, anchored by an amber pilot banner along the header.

### 50-Day Roadmap Timeline
- **Spine Line:** Hairline vertical or horizontal track in `#D4CEBF`.
- **Nodes:** Active phase uses solid `#073F30` circle with white core; completed uses `#0D5C46`; upcoming uses `#FBFAF7` with a `1.5px solid #D4CEBF` ring.
- **Phase Modules:** Grouped in sets (e.g., Days 1–15: Clarity & Positioning, Days 16–35: Outreach & Sprints, Days 36–50: Offer Closing).

### Comparison Columns
- **Architecture:** 2 to 3 parallel columns with subtle alternating row fills in `#FBFAF7` and `#F4F1EA`.
- **Feature Rows:** Cell division maintained via `1px solid #E5E0D5`. Step Right column is elevated using a delicate forest outline and neutral background tint.

### FAQ Accordions
- **Container:** Unbordered top and bottom rows using crisp dividers (`1px solid #E5E0D5`).
- **Trigger:** DM Sans Headline-SM, full-width click area, transitioning smoothly via chevron rotation with no layout jump.
- **Content:** Manrope Body-MD in `#2D3748`, with `space-md` bottom padding.

### Form Inputs
- **Field:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D4CEBF`, radius `4px`, padding `12px 16px`, text `#18221E`.
- **Focus:** Outline None, border `1.5px solid #073F30`, ring `2px rgba(7, 63, 48, 0.1)`.
