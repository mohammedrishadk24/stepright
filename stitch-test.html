Interview preparation

About Stepright

Our purpose

Stepright offers practical career mentorship for graduates and early-career job seekers, helping them approach their job search with greater clarity and confidence.

The programme focuses on resumes, job applications, recruiter outreach and interview preparation, with guidance tailored to each person's goals.

The Programme

50 days, one clear direction

A five-phase plan that takes you from setting job-search goals to interview preparation.

P1
Start Day 1 to 2
What we do

A 30 to 60 minute one-on-one call to discuss your background, goals and job-search challenges. Complete a short form beforehand.

What you leave with

A clear target (1 or 2 roles), an honest diagnosis, your first action list.

P2
Foundation Day 3 to 7
What we do

Improve your resume and LinkedIn profile with feedback, and set up an application tracker.

What you leave with

A job-ready resume and LinkedIn profile; application tracker.

P3
Build Day 8 to 20
What we do

Identify skill gaps for your target role and build a focused learning plan and target-company list, using free resources where possible.

What you leave with

A focused skill plan and a target company list.

P4
Apply Day 21 to 40
What we do

Build a routine for tailored applications, recruiter outreach and follow-ups. Review applications and messages, and share relevant openings.

What you leave with

Applications that stand out; a habit you can keep using.

P5
Interview Day 41 to 50
What we do

Prepare from job descriptions using AI-assisted practice questions and a mock interview; cover salary-negotiation basics.

What you leave with

Interview confidence and a clear approach to offers.
