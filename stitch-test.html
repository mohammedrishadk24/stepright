The Problem

Searching for a job is harder than it should be.

Most job seekers are not short of effort. They apply to dozens of openings, hear nothing back, and slowly lose confidence. In most cases the issue is not talent. It is that nobody ever taught them how the process actually works.

Does any of this sound familiar?

1

You send applications every day and rarely get a reply, and you don't know why.

2

Your resume looks like everyone else's, or you're not sure what to highlight and what to leave out.

3

You don't know how to approach an HR or a recruiter, or what to write in that email or message.

4

LinkedIn feels confusing, and you're unsure whether your profile helps or hurts you.

5

You don't know which skills, courses or companies are worth your time, so you jump between random videos and certificates.

6

Interviews make you nervous because you've never practised them properly.

7

You're applying alone, with nobody to tell you what's going wrong.

lightbulb
Why this matters

Every month without a plan costs confidence, not just time. A job seeker who applies with a clear target, a tailored resume and a follow-up habit has a very different search from one who sends the same resume everywhere and waits.

The Programme

50 days, one clear direction

A structured, one-on-one mentorship for graduates and early-career job seekers. You get a personal plan, honest feedback on your applications, and someone to talk to along the way.

P1
Start Day 1 to 2
What we do

One-on-one introduction call (30 to 60 minutes). We understand your background, goals, and what's been going wrong. You fill a short form beforehand so we use the time well.

What you leave with

A clear target (1 or 2 roles), an honest diagnosis, your first action list.

P2
Foundation Day 1 to 7
What we do

Resume rebuilt or improved, with feedback rounds. LinkedIn profile fixed. A simple tracker set up to log every application.

What you leave with

A job-ready resume and LinkedIn profile; application tracker.

P3
Build Day 8 to 20
What we do

Skill gaps identified for your target role. If a course or small project is truly needed, we suggest the right one (free where possible). We build your list of target companies.

What you leave with

A focused skill plan and a target company list.

P4
Apply Day 21 to 40
What we do

A daily routine: tailored applications, outreach to HRs and recruiters, and proper follow-ups. We share openings we come across and review your applications and messages.

What you leave with

Applications that stand out; a habit you can keep using.

P5
Interview Day 41 to 50
What we do

Reading a job description, preparing answers, AI-assisted practice questions, mock interview, and how to respond when a call comes. Basics of salary negotiation.

What you leave with

Interview confidence and a clear approach to offers.

An Honest Promise — What we promise, and what we don't

The Reality of Job Search

We do not promise you a job. No honest mentor can. Hiring depends on the market, the company and your own effort. What we promise is a clear plan, honest feedback, our real time and attention, and a process that makes your search far stronger than it is today.
