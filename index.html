The programme

A structured 50-day career plan.

The programme covers five stages, from identifying suitable roles to preparing for interviews and planning your next steps. The sequence can be adjusted to reflect your priorities and progress.

  1. 01 Days 1–7Review your background and goals

    Review your education, experience, strengths and career interests. Identify relevant roles and assess your current resume and LinkedIn profile.

    By the endA shortlist of roles to explore and priorities for improving your application materials.

  2. 02 Days 8–20Prepare your application materials

    Improve how you present your skills and experience. Set up an application tracker and identify relevant learning resources where needed.

    By the endUpdated application materials and a clear system for organising your job search.

  3. 03 Days 21–35Plan and make targeted applications

    Identify relevant vacancies, tailor applications and practise writing professional messages to recruiters and employers.

    By the endTemplates you can adapt for applications and professional outreach.

  4. 04 Days 36–45Prepare for interviews

    Prepare for common interview questions, practise describing your experience and receive feedback on your answers.

    By the endA structured approach to answering interview questions and priorities for further practice.

  5. 05 Days 46–50Review and move forward

    Review your progress, address any remaining challenges and plan how to continue your job search after the programme.

    By the endA progress summary and priorities for the month ahead.

Already applying? You can work on applications before Day 21. The stages can be reordered to address your immediate priorities.

A good match matters

Is this programme a good fit for you?

The mentorship is best suited to people who are ready to take an active role in their job search.

StepRight may be suitable if you...

  • Are a recent graduate or early-career job seeker seeking help to identify suitable roles.
  • Would value feedback on your resume, LinkedIn profile or application approach.
  • Can make time for applications, preparation and agreed follow-up.
  • Are prepared to manage your own job search with guidance and feedback.

StepRight may not be suitable if you...

  • Need someone to submit job applications on your behalf.
  • Are seeking a guaranteed job, interview, referral or placement.
  • Cannot make time for your own applications and preparation.
