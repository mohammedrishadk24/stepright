The programme

50 days. Five clear steps.

We'll work on the parts of your job search that need attention most. The timeline is a guide, so it can be adjusted to your goals and progress.

  1. Days 1–7

    01. Get clear on your direction

    Talk through your background, identify suitable roles, and review your resume and LinkedIn profile.

    Your first stepA role shortlist, improvement priorities and a simple action plan.

  2. Days 8–20

    02. Build your job-search toolkit

    Improve how you present your experience, set up a tracker and identify useful learning resources.

    Your next stepA stronger profile and a practical application routine.

  3. Days 21–35

    03. Apply with more purpose

    Find relevant openings, tailor selected applications and learn how to approach recruiters professionally.

    Your next stepApplication and outreach templates you can use independently.

  4. Days 36–45

    04. Prepare for interviews

    Practise common questions, explain your experience clearly and receive feedback on your answers.

    Your next stepAn interview-preparation checklist and feedback.

  5. Days 46–50

    05. Review and move forward

    Look at your progress, understand what's still challenging and plan your next month of applications.

    Your next stepA career progress summary and your next steps.

Already ready to apply? You won't have to wait until Day 21. We can adapt the order to what you need most.

A few things you may be wondering

Questions? We've got answers.

If there's something else you'd like to know, you can always ask us directly before joining.

Will StepRight find me a job?

No. StepRight is a mentorship service, not a recruitment agency. We help you strengthen your approach, but interviews and hiring decisions are made by employers.

What if I already have a resume?

That's useful. We'll review what you have and focus on the changes that matter for your target roles. You won't have to start from scratch.

Do I need to buy courses or extra services?

No paid courses are required. If we identify a skill gap, we'll suggest suitable free learning resources first.

When can I message my mentor?

We'll discuss the available support and response times with you before you decide to enrol.

What happens after the 50 days?

We'll review your progress and discuss practical next steps. Any ongoing support or follow-up will be explained before you enrol.

What if I change my mind?

We haven't finalised a refund policy. Please ask us about the applicable refund and cancellation terms before making a payment.
