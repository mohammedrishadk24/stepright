WHY STEP RIGHT

Practical guidance, without the big promises.

Step Right grew from helping friends and juniors with resumes, job applications, recruiter outreach and interview preparation—and recognising how useful that support can be.

The aim is to make practical career guidance more accessible, with individual attention, honest feedback and steps you can actually act on.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions you might have.

Still unsure? Get in touch before making a decision. We'll be honest about whether the programme is a good fit.

Will Step Right get me a job?

No. We don't promise jobs, interviews or referrals. We help you strengthen your approach and guide you through the process.

What if I have little or no work experience?

That's okay. We can work on presenting your education, projects and skills clearly, and identify practical next steps for your target roles.

Will I need to buy courses?

Not by default. We will discuss courses or projects only if they address a genuine skill gap, and free options will be considered first.

How much time will I need?

The time required will vary. Plan for regular, focused effort on applications, learning and follow-ups throughout the 50 days.

What happens after 50 days?

The programme ends after 50 days. Any alumni group or ongoing support should be confirmed with the mentor before enrolment; continued one-to-one mentorship is not automatically included.

Is my information kept private?

Your resume and personal details are used for mentorship and won't be shared without your permission, except where required by law. Never share passwords or one-time passcodes.

What if the programme isn't right for me?

Let's have a quick conversation before you pay. If you decide after the introduction call that the programme isn't right for you, the pilot fee will be refunded in full.
