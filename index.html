Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the Step Right 50-day sprint.

Will you get me a job?

No. We do not place you in a job and will never promise that we can. What we do is help you build the resume, habits, story, and interview readiness that give you the strongest possible chance.

Who will I be speaking to?

You will be working directly with Mohammed, a working professional with real-world experience who has helped peers through similar early-career challenges.

What if I have little or no experience?

That is completely fine. The programme is designed for fresh graduates and early-career job seekers, and the focus is on your potential, skills, and progression.

How much time will this take?

You should expect to invest around 1 to 2 hours a day in applications, follow-up, and preparation. The progress depends on consistency and effort.

Do you provide referrals or guaranteed interviews?

No. We do not offer back-door referrals or guarantees. The programme is built around honest mentorship, structured guidance, and practical job-search improvements.
