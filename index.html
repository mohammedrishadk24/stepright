The programme

50 days. Five clear steps.

We'll work on the parts of your job search that need attention most. The timeline is a guide, so it can be adjusted to your goals and progress.

Days 1–7

Get clear on your direction

Talk through your background, identify suitable roles, and review your resume and LinkedIn profile.

You leave with: a role shortlist, improvement priorities and a simple action plan.

Days 8–20

Build your job-search toolkit

Improve how you present your experience, set up a tracker and identify useful learning resources.

You leave with: a stronger profile and a practical application routine.

Days 21–35

Apply with more purpose

Find relevant openings, tailor selected applications and learn how to approach recruiters professionally.

You leave with: application and outreach templates you can use independently.

Days 36–45

Prepare for interviews

Practise common questions, explain your experience clearly and receive feedback on your answers.

You leave with: an interview-preparation checklist and feedback.

Days 46–50

Review and move forward

Look at your progress, understand what's still challenging and plan your next month of applications.

You leave with: a career progress summary and your next steps.

Already ready to apply? You won't have to wait until Day 21. We can adapt the order to what you need most.

A good match matters

Is StepRight right for you?

We want you to join only if the programme can genuinely help with your goals.

It could be a good fit if you...

  • Are a recent graduate or early-career job seeker.
  • Need direction, structure or feedback on your applications.
  • Want to improve your resume, LinkedIn or interview skills.
  • Can make time to practise and apply consistently.

It may not be right if you...

  • Want someone else to submit all your applications.
  • Need specialist preparation for exams or regulated professions.
  • Cannot commit time to your own job search.

Do I need any work experience?

No. The programme is designed for recent graduates and early-career job seekers. We'll help you present your projects, education, volunteering and relevant skills honestly.

What if I already have a resume?

That's useful. We'll review what you have and focus on the changes that matter for your target roles. You won't have to start from scratch.

How much time will I need?

The time you'll need depends on your goals and current job search. Talk through what you can realistically set aside during your initial conversation.

Do I need to buy courses or extra services?

No paid courses are required. If we identify a skill gap, we'll suggest suitable free learning resources first.
