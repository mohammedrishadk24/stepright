Programme terms

These terms explain the basis on which StepRight provides mentorship and what is expected from participants. Please read them before enrolling.

Important. The programme scope, support inclusions, and refund or rescheduling rules should be confirmed with StepRight before payment is made.

What the programme is

StepRight offers practical career mentorship for graduates and early-career job seekers. The programme is structured to support direction-setting, application improvement, interview preparation and reflection on next steps. The exact start date, session count, deliverables and support limits should be confirmed before enrolment.

What it is not

StepRight is not a recruitment or placement agency. We do not guarantee interviews, referrals, a job offer, a particular salary or an employment outcome. You remain responsible for the accuracy of the information you share, your job applications and your decisions.

Enrolment and payment

Any fee and payment process should be confirmed in writing before enrolment. Payment is not collected through this website, and StepRight may use a separate channel to receive payment for the programme.

Your participation

You are expected to share accurate information, respond to agreed review points and take responsibility for your own applications and career decisions. Support is most effective when you engage consistently and keep to the agreed schedule.

Support and communication

Support may include review of materials, guidance on applications, interview preparation and feedback on progress. The exact scope, communication channel and response windows should be clarified before you enrol.

Refunds, cancellations and rescheduling

Any refund, cancellation, rescheduling or exception policy should be agreed in writing before an enrolment is confirmed. We recommend confirming the specific terms before making a payment.

Privacy and data protection

StepRight handles personal data in line with the privacy notice. If you want to contact the Data Protection Officer or exercise any privacy rights, please use the details on that page.

Contact

For questions about the programme or data protection, contact StepRight through the WhatsApp link on the main page or the DPO contact details in the privacy notice.
