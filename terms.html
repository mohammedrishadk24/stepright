Programme terms

These terms explain how StepRight provides mentorship and what participants are expected to do. Please read them before you enrol.

Important. The programme scope, support inclusions, and refund or rescheduling rules should be confirmed with StepRight before payment is made.

What the programme is

StepRight offers one-to-one career mentorship for graduates and early-career job seekers. The programme covers career planning, application improvement, interview preparation and next steps. Please confirm the start date, number of sessions, deliverables and support limits before enrolment.

What it is not

StepRight is not a recruitment or placement agency. We do not guarantee interviews, referrals, a job offer, a particular salary or an employment outcome. You remain responsible for the accuracy of the information you share, your job applications and your decisions.

Enrolment and payment

The current programme fee is shown on the main website. Before enrolment, StepRight will confirm the fee, included support and payment method with you in writing. Payment is not collected through this website.

Your participation

You are expected to provide accurate information, take part in agreed reviews and remain responsible for your applications and career decisions. Consistent participation and following the agreed schedule will help you make the most of the support.

Support and communication

Support may include reviews of your career materials, application guidance, interview preparation and progress feedback. Please confirm the scope of support, communication method and response times before you enrol.

Refunds, cancellations and rescheduling

Any refund, cancellation, rescheduling or exception terms should be agreed in writing before your enrolment is confirmed. Please review these terms before making a payment.

Privacy and data protection

StepRight handles personal data in line with the privacy notice. If you want to contact the Data Protection Officer or exercise any privacy rights, please use the details on that page.

Contact

For questions about the programme or data protection, contact StepRight through the WhatsApp link on the main page or the DPO contact details in the privacy notice.
