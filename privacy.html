Privacy notice

We understand that your resume, career history, contact details and job-search journey can be personal. This page explains how we handle personal data when you enquire, enrol or take part in StepRight.

Data protection contact. If you want to ask about your data, request a copy, correct information or exercise a right under applicable privacy laws, contact the Data Protection Officer using the details in the final section of this page.

What we collect

When you enquire or enrol, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, education and employment background, resume, target roles, and any details you share in calls, messages, forms or programme materials. We may also keep basic payment and programme records needed to administer the mentorship.

Why we use it

We use personal data to respond to queries, assess whether StepRight is suitable, provide mentorship, review career materials, monitor progress, keep records of our service and manage payments, refunds or programme administration.

Who can access it

The mentor or team member supporting your programme may access personal data required to deliver the service. We may use communication, document and scheduling tools such as WhatsApp, Google Workspace and similar services, which have their own privacy practices. We do not sell personal information or share your resume with recruiters without your explicit permission.

Your rights

You may have the right to ask for access to your personal data, request correction, ask for deletion where appropriate, object to particular processing, request restriction of processing, or ask for a copy of your data in a portable format. If you wish to withdraw consent for communications or data processing where consent is the legal basis, let us know and we will act promptly.

How long it is kept

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the mentorship, administration and applicable legal obligations. Some records may need to be retained where law or accounting requirements apply. When data is no longer needed, we will delete or anonymise it when appropriate.

Contact the Data Protection Officer

If you have a question about your data, want to opt out of ongoing contact, exercise a privacy right or raise a concern, contact the StepRight Data Protection Officer (DPO):

  • Email: hello@stepright.live
  • WhatsApp: message the team

If you prefer, you can also contact us through the WhatsApp link on the main website and ask to speak with the DPO.
