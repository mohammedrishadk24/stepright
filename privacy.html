Privacy notice

Your resume, career history, contact details and job-search information are personal. This notice explains how StepRight handles your information when you enquire, enrol or take part in the programme.

Data protection contact. If you want to ask about your data, request a copy, correct information or exercise a right under applicable privacy laws, contact the Data Protection Officer using the details in the final section of this page.

What we collect

When you enquire or enrol, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, education and employment background, resume, target roles, and any details you share in calls, messages, forms or programme materials. We may also keep basic payment and programme records needed to administer the mentorship.

Why we use it

We use personal data to respond to queries, assess whether StepRight is suitable, provide mentorship, review career materials, monitor progress, keep records of our service and manage payments, refunds or programme administration.

Who can access it

Your mentor may access the personal data needed to provide the service. We may use communication, document and scheduling tools such as WhatsApp, Google Workspace and similar services, each of which has its own privacy practices. We do not sell personal information or share your resume with recruiters without your explicit permission.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access your personal data, request corrections or deletion where appropriate, object to or restrict certain uses, or request a copy in a portable format. If you want to withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing, contact us and we will respond promptly.

How long it is kept

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the mentorship, administer the programme and meet legal obligations. We may need to retain some records to meet legal or accounting requirements. When information is no longer needed, we will delete or anonymise it where appropriate.

Contact the Data Protection Officer

To ask a question about your information, opt out of ongoing contact, exercise a privacy right or raise a concern, contact the StepRight Data Protection Officer (DPO):

  • Email: hello@stepright.live
  • WhatsApp: message StepRight

If you prefer, you can also contact us through the WhatsApp link on the main website and ask to speak with the DPO.
