Privacy note

We understand that your resume, contact information and job search can be personal. This note explains how we handle the information you choose to share.

Draft for review. Please check that every statement on this page matches StepRight's actual practices and applicable requirements before publishing it as a final policy.

What we collect

When you enquire or enrol, we may receive your name, phone number, email address, education and employment background, resume, target roles, and information you share in calls or messages. We also keep basic payment and programme records.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond to your questions, assess whether StepRight is suitable, deliver mentorship, review materials, track your progress and manage payments or refunds.

Who can access it

The mentor handling your programme may access the information necessary to support you. We may use communication and document services such as WhatsApp and Google Workspace; those services have their own privacy practices. We do not sell your information or share your resume with recruiters without your permission.

How long it is kept

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the mentorship, administration and applicable legal obligations. You can request access, correction or deletion by contacting us; some records may need to be retained where law requires it.

Contact us

If you have a question about your data or want us to stop contacting you, message us using the WhatsApp link on the main website.
